# Week 4 Business Bending — Career Experiment

## 1. Problem in my words

Many university students have different interests but do not know what to do with them. They can use AI, talk to professors, search on LinkedIn, or do internships, but these things are not connected. A student can finish an experience and still not know what they learned about themselves or what they should try next.

My idea is to help students learn from small real work experiences and use that learning to choose their next experiment.

## 2. Exact user

My user is a Mexican university student who is around the middle of their degree. They have different interests and may have some experience from internships, projects, or part-time jobs, but they are still not sure what direction to take.

The product should be simple, mobile-friendly, and easy to use.

## 3. Success definition

Before the module closes, the user can:

1. Choose a small real-world project.
2. Complete the project.
3. Add what they did.
4. Receive feedback about that specific project.
5. Reflect on what they liked and did not like.
6. See 2 or 3 possible next experiments.

The system does not choose a career for the student.

## 4. Mockup

The product will have a simple flow:

Home → Choose a project → Do the project → Add your work → Get feedback → Reflect → Choose your next experiment
